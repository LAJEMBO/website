Safety and trust are built into the rental journey.

LAJEMBO is designed to reduce unnecessary exposure of customer information while creating a clearer, more accountable way for renters and property providers to work together.

Verified properties

Properties must complete the required LAJEMBO verification journey before they can become publicly listed. Verification helps reduce false, duplicate and unauthorized listings.

Identity review

Identity approval currently uses documented manual review of supporting evidence. Automated government-record verification is not connected. Submitting an ID number alone does not verify identity.

Protected identity and location

Customer identity and exact property-location details are disclosed only at the appropriate stage of a protected rental journey. Public browsing does not expose protected customer records or exact addresses.

Account verification

LAJEMBO uses account and mobile-number verification controls to help confirm that protected journeys are being accessed by the registered customer.

Controlled staff access

Operational access is role-based. Sensitive customer, property and transaction information is intended for authorized personnel whose work requires it—not general staff access.

Recorded activity and accountability

Important verification, operational and transaction actions are designed to retain accountable records and audit trails, including separation of duties where approval controls require it.

Payments and transaction records

LAJEMBO keeps payment and transaction activity tied to the relevant customer and rental journey. Required charges and approvals are presented through controlled platform processes rather than informal off-platform changes.

Documents and rental records

Rental documents, receipts and transaction history are kept within the customer journey so important records remain associated with the correct rental.

Fraud and circumvention controls

Attempts to misuse accounts, bypass protected processes, misrepresent a property or circumvent LAJEMBO safeguards may be reviewed and can result in restrictions or suspension.

Support when something is wrong

Customers can raise safety, verification, property, viewing or transaction concerns through LAJEMBO support. Reports are tied to the relevant journey so they can be reviewed with the available records.
